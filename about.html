About

Advancing intelligent systems responsibly

I am a Presidential Research Fellow at Georgia Southern University, working in the area of Health Explainable AI. My research focuses on building mathematically grounded, interpretable machine learning and deep learning models for clinical applications — with a particular interest in ocular disease recognition, survival analysis, and privacy-preserving edge deployment.

I hold an M.S.E. in Data Science from the Whiting School of Engineering, Johns Hopkins University, where I was advised by Dr. Brian S. Caffo. I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at SASTRA University. I am also an Intel Software Innovator and hold certifications from Intel, Microsoft, and NVIDIA.
