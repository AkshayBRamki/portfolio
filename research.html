Project 01
Johns Hopkins University Georgia Southern University

Explainable, Privacy-Preserving Edge-Deployable Ocular Disease Recognition

A progressive multi-stage research effort to build an ocular disease recognition system that is not just accurate, but explainable, equitable, and deployable in real-world clinical settings globally. Starting from binary cataract classification (98.36% accuracy, published in Applied AI Letters), extended to 8-class multi-label classification on ODIR-5K via the custom RETI-NET 360 hybrid multi-stream architecture. Explainability via Grad-CAM is used as a layer-by-layer failure audit mechanism — not as decoration. End goal: a device-agnostic, on-device inference system with no patient data leaving the device, generalizing across global populations.

Project 02
Mirakle Integrated Health Centre, Pollachi

Equitable Cancer Prognosis Framework for Real-World Integrative Care

A long-term collaborative project tackling one of oncology's most persistent gaps — the lack of a unified, fair, and clinically usable prognosis tool for real-world cancer care. In collaboration with Mirakle Integrated Health Centre, developing a three-pillar framework: a dropout prediction model, a survival and prognostic model integrating patient-reported outcomes and longitudinal lab data, and a transparent bedside metabolic risk score derived from standard blood panels (CRP, LDH, ferritin, D-dimer, CEA) linked to personalized integrative care pathways. The framework is equity-aware, with explicit fairness audits across age, sex, cancer type, and socioeconomic subgroups. A comprehensive review article covering 100+ papers has been submitted.
